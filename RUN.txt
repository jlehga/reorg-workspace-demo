After Sign in you land on Workspace home. Use sidebar Sign out to return
to the login screen.

OPENAI_API_KEY / LLM_API_KEY is optional. Without either, a deterministic demo
extractor runs (fine for the walkthrough). With a key, interpretation uses a live LLM.
