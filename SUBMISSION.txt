What the prototype demonstrates
-------------------------------
This Streamlit app is a Reorg Workspace portal. After Sign in, operators open
or start a Reorg Case and walk a cost-center reorg: natural-language intake →
validated case → approval → task execution → reconciliation and audit trail.
Scenario A covers the happy path; optional controls show blocked reporting on
failed GL reconciliation, and Scenario B surfaces ambiguity and validation
failures.

For architecture, trade-offs, and scope, see docs/design.md. To clone, install,
and follow the ~60-second demo path (Streamlit on port 8765), use RUN.txt.
Optional LLM_API_KEY lets the app call a live LLM for freeform text interpretation
only; without it, a built-in demo parser is used. The model does not approve or
execute changes.
