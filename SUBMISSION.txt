What the prototype demonstrates
-------------------------------
This Streamlit app walks an HR/Finance operator through a cost-center reorg:
natural-language intake → validated case → approval → task execution →
reconciliation and audit trail. Scenario A covers the happy path; optional
controls show blocked reporting on failed GL reconciliation and Scenario B
surfaces ambiguity / validation failures.

OPENAI_API_KEY is optional; without it, a deterministic demo extractor is used.
