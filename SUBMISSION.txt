What the prototype demonstrates
-------------------------------
Reorg Workspace is an operator portal. After Sign in, open or start a
Reorg Case and walk a cost-center reorg: natural-language intake →
validated case → approval → task execution → reconciliation and audit.
Scenario A is the happy path; optional controls show blocked reporting
on failed GL reconciliation; Scenario B surfaces ambiguity and
validation failures.

Architecture, trade-offs, and scope: docs/design.md.

Optional LLM_API_KEY enables live LLM interpretation of freeform text;
without it, a built-in demo parser is used. Optional LLM_MODEL /
LLM_BASE_URL. The model does not approve or execute changes.
